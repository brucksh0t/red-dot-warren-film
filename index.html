Since Bastille Day 1999

It began
as Raymakers.

Red Dot opened at 321 Warren Street as Raymakers, a Belgian frites, burgers and mussels bar. The mussels and the frites never left the menu.

  • Raymakers opens at 321 Warren: frites, burgers, mussels.

  • Red Dot takes over next door and adds a dance floor, a screening room and a garden with an outdoor fire.

  • Dinner five nights, brunch on weekends, and years of Halloween parties, fundraisers and weddings.
